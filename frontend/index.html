What a rank can—and cannot—tell you

The score combines human expression-association evidence with the magnitude of a mouse marker-screen effect. MHCII, CD40 and PD-L1 are separate endpoints.

It is not a probability, a therapeutic recommendation, or a measured T-cell-function effect.

Patient-resampling ranges describe stability in this small cohort. They do not establish independent predictive performance.
