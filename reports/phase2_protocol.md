# Phase 2 descriptive protocol

User authorized descriptive Phase 2 following the Phase 1 predictive NO-GO. No functional prediction or causal claim is authorized by this analysis. Configuration frozen before association results; config and source hashes are recorded at runtime.

Use the 11 LUNG_T primary-tumor samples and explicit patient mapping. Retain published annotations as reference-supported labels; marker checks are consistency checks, not independent validation. Do not select T cells using inhibitory signature values. Published QC bounds are cohort-specific (200–10000 genes; 100–150000 UMI; mitochondrial fraction <=20%; Kim et al., https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16164-1). These data already underwent source QC. Reapply transparent bounds and inspect per-sample distributions. Assess doublets per sample with Scrublet (expected rate 5% is an assumption); main analysis excludes calls and sensitivity retains them. Additional sensitivity excludes sample-wise extreme low genes/high mitochondrial outliers using 3 median absolute deviations.

Primary endpoint: arithmetic mean of six inhibitory/dysfunction-associated genes after cell-wise log1p(counts/total*10000), then equal-weight cell mean within all annotated T lymphocytes per patient. This is an expression proxy, not measured dysfunction. Markers are literature-supported, analyst-defined panels, not a validated clinical score: Guo et al. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-018-0045-3 and Kim et al. above. Alternative endpoint is the cytotoxic panel. Macrophage lipid and antigen-presentation panels provide descriptive context, not suppression labels. No data-driven signature tuning.

Gene predictor: mean normalized log expression in published Alveolar Mac and mo-Mac cells per patient. Candidate eligibility: detection >=10% in retained macrophages; nonzero patient mean in at least six patients; nonconstant predictor. Exclude mitochondrial/ribosomal genes and all signature/annotation panel genes to avoid circular candidate validation. Gene symbols are source feature identifiers; obtain HGNC stable IDs where a unique approved-symbol mapping is available, leaving unresolved IDs blank.

Primary model is standardized patient-level OLS, with HC3 robust standard errors and t-based inference (n-2 degrees of freedom). BH correction across all eligible genes for the primary endpoint. Separate BH families for sensitivity models. With n=11, results are exploratory and unstable estimates are expected. No cell-level p-values.

Composition: macrophage state proportions use all retained Alveolar Mac + mo-Mac cells as denominator. S_i=sum_k p_ik*mu_ik for a gene on the same log-normalized scale. Composition-only=sum_k p_ik*mean_i(mu_ik); standardized-within-state=sum_k mean_i(p_ik)*mu_ik; residual interaction is reported. No imputation for absent states. Adjust primary association for mo-Mac proportion; test standardized-within-state predictor separately.

Sensitivities: retain doublets, stricter sample-adaptive QC, exclude ADC(Double), age-adjustment and sex-adjustment separately, macrophage library-size adjustment, CD8-only endpoint (published subtype labels), cytotoxic endpoint, and leave-one-patient-out effects. Do not fit a large multivariable model to 11 patients. No cohort integration or aggressive batch correction: sample/capture and patient are confounded; report this limitation and library-size sensitivity.

Metadata-only amendment before association results: source broad T labels include unclassified and NK subtypes. Add a strict-annotation sensitivity excluding NA, NK and Undetermined subtypes from the T score. A sample lacks alveolar macrophages; standardized-within-state analyses omit missing-state patients and report their effective n. Composition-only references average observed state means, with no imputed patient state. Small nonzero state populations remain a precision limitation.

Runtime compatibility: pin scikit-learn 1.7.2 with Scanpy 1.11.5 after newer scikit-learn returned sparse-array objects incompatible with Scanpy's neighbor code. Scrublet uses exact nearest neighbors, avoiding approximate-neighbor implementation variability. This choice was made before association results.
