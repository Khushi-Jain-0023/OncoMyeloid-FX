Nor pool their CD4 infection-antigen activation with Marelli CD8 proliferation without a justified endpoint model. Positive-selected follow-up cannot estimate genome-wide precision.

## Protocol if reopened
1. Freeze one functional endpoint, intervention direction, context and timepoint. Keep proliferation, cytokine output and killing separate.
2. Restrict evaluation to actually tested eligible genes with controls. Untested genes remain unknown.
3. Group publication families, shared controls, donor replicates, guides and batches before feature construction.
4. Hold out whole independent families; remove their labels and derived features from development. Audit overlapping prior databases. A prospective dataset is preferable because published qualitative outcomes were inspected during this audit.
5. Compare transcriptomic association with transparent evidence integration; ligand methods only on compatible ligand subsets.
6. Use within-assay rank correlation for continuous outcomes, or AUPRC and prespecified precision@k for valid binary labels. Bootstrap independent biological units/studies, not cells or technical wells.
7. Test composition, signature, annotation, evidence-tier and study-exclusion sensitivity. Too few independent studies means descriptive validation only.
8. Freeze the protocol before producing rankings; report uncertainty and negative results.
