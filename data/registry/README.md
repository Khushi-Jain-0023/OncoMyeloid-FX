# Registry interpretation

Phase 1 records are feasibility records, not ranking inputs. Perturbation rows are qualitative leads with verified paper/figure provenance. Blank stable gene IDs, effect sizes and biological_n are unresolved, not zero, and prevent quantitative ranking. Mouse evidence is classified indirect relative to human lung cancer even where macrophages are directly perturbed.

Direction refers to the T-cell readout, not therapeutic benefit. no_detected_decrease does not establish equivalence or a null effect. Combined interventions, drug screens and study-level exclusions are documented in the reports rather than assigned invented single-gene labels. Split groups conservatively combine related papers.
