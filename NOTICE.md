# Source attribution and redistribution

This release includes derived research results and public source snapshots for reproducibility. Source data retain their original provider terms; this release does not relicense them or claim ownership. No new open-source license has been assigned to project code. Public publication/licensing is a separate owner decision.

- GSE131907: Kim et al., Nature Communications (2020), DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-16164-1. GEO accession and download URLs are recorded in README.md and the dataset registry. The full raw count matrix and H5AD caches are omitted from the compact release; derived patient-level summaries are included.
- Kiritsy et al., eLife (2021), DOI 10.7554/eLife.65109: Figure 1 source workbook and article XML, unchanged public snapshots in data/sources/phase3/. Publisher terms and attribution apply. The related 65110 study remains in the evidence registry, not a separate validation family.
- MGI/Alliance: HOM_MouseHumanSequence.rpt snapshot, from https://www.informatics.jax.org/homology.shtml. Provider attribution and terms apply.
- HGNC complete set: snapshot in work/hgnc_complete_set.txt, from https://www.genenames.org/download/archive/. Source URL/hash are retained in the manifests; HGNC attribution and terms apply.

No private credentials or controlled-access records are included. Stable public patient labels are research units, not personal identities. The release is descriptive research and does not provide clinical recommendations.
